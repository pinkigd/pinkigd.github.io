My method

Four steps, each one building on the last.

Step 01 · Discovery

Understand

I listen first: your users, your business and the constraints, and name the real problem before anything gets drawn.

  • User research
  • Competitor audit
  • Problem statement
Step 02 · Flows

Structure

Journeys and wireframes before any pixels, so the logic holds up when the visuals arrive.

  • User flows
  • Wireframes
  • Information architecture
Step 03 · Interface

Design

High-fidelity UI on a reusable system: type, colour, components and states that stay coherent.

  • UI design
  • Design system
  • Interactive prototype
Step 04 · Validation

Prove

I prototype, test and refine, then hand off clean, documented files developers can build from.

  • Usability testing
  • Refinements
  • Developer handoff
